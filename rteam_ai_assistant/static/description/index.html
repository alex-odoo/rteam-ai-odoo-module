60 seconds from install to first question

1. Install the module

Install Rteam AI Assistant from Apps. A new Rteam AI menu appears in your Odoo navigation.

2. Generate a token

Open Settings → Rteam AI → Connect Telegram Bot. Pick a scope (read-only or read & write). Click Generate Token.

3. Open the bot

Click Open @RteamAI_bot on Telegram. The bot starts with your token pre-filled — no copy-paste needed.

4. Ask anything

Type or speak your question. The bot reads your Odoo schema, runs the right queries, and answers in seconds.

Privacy & security first

Scoped tokens

Choose read-only or read & write scope per connection. Rotate or revoke anytime. Token hash is stored — the raw token is shown once, then gone.

Your data stays yours

The assistant answers questions by reading data on demand. Nothing is persistently copied out of your Odoo. Conversation logs are stored back in your own Odoo instance, visible under Rteam AI → Conversations.

Uninstall = disconnect

Remove the module and all tokens are automatically revoked. No orphan connections, no lingering access.

Audit trail

Every question, every answer, every write action is logged in Odoo with timestamp, user, duration, and tokens consumed.
